HanaLima is an unofficial GUI for Lima. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Lima project or the CNCF.

Hana lima is Hawaiian for "handmade".

Uses SwiftTerm (MIT) and Yams (MIT). Lima (Apache-2.0) is not bundled; HanaLima runs the limactl installed on your Mac.
